Sushidoku Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

This policy explains what information Sushidoku, the puzzle game by Gamedelics ("we", "us"), collects on Android and iPhone, why, who it's shared with, and the choices you have. Questions: gamedelics@gmail.com.

The short version

  • We don't ask for your name, email, phone number or address, and the game has no login.
  • Your progress (levels, coins, settings) is saved on your phone.
  • The game is free because of ads. Ad networks and our measurement services receive device information, such as an advertising ID, as described below, and in the European Economic Area, the UK and US states that require it, only with your consent.
  • The leaderboard uses an anonymous account with the name you pick (or a default one), your sushi and your score. You can delete it in the game at any time.

Information on your phone

Your game progress, coins, settings and saved boards are stored on your phone. We don't receive them, except your leaderboard score as described below. Uninstalling the game deletes them.

The leaderboard (Unity Gaming Services)

When the leaderboard opens (after your third customer is served), the game creates an anonymous account with Unity Gaming Services, run by Unity Technologies. It holds:

  • a random player ID (no name, email or login);
  • your leaderboard name: a default one such as "Salmon nigiri 214", or one you choose, which other players can see;
  • your avatar (a sushi from the game) and your score (customers served), which other players can see;
  • reports you make about other players' names, and reports about yours, which we review to keep names appropriate.

You can change your name and avatar in the Sushi Menu. To delete your leaderboard account (name, avatar and score): Sushi Menu ▸ your name ▸ Delete my leaderboard data. Your progress on your phone stays.

Ads (LevelPlay and its ad networks)

Sushidoku shows banner ads, ads between levels, and reward videos you choose to watch, through Unity LevelPlay (ironSource), with the ad networks ironSource Ads, Unity Ads and Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and prevent fraud, these companies receive information such as your device's advertising ID (Google's advertising ID, or Apple's IDFA if you allow tracking), IP address and approximate location derived from it, device model and settings, and how you interact with ads. Depending on your consent, ads may be personalised.

No ads are shown before level 10. Remove Ads (a one-time purchase) removes the banner and the ads between levels; reward videos stay available if you choose to watch them.

Measurement and crash reports

  • Google Firebase Analytics (Google) receives how the game is used, such as levels started and completed, tools used and ads seen, with an app instance ID, the device's advertising ID (on iPhone only if you allow tracking) and device information, so we can improve the game. It collects nothing until your consent is settled, and in the EEA and UK only with your consent.
  • Google Firebase Crashlytics (Google) receives crash reports: the device model, operating system, the state of the game when it crashed, and a crash installation ID. We use them only to fix crashes.
  • Tenjin measures where installs come from (for example, which ad campaign) and the game's ad revenue. It receives the advertising ID when allowed, device information and events such as reaching a level. It starts only with your consent where consent is required, and after a US state opt-out it stops receiving the advertising ID.

Purchases

Remove Ads is bought through Google Play or Apple's App Store, which handle the payment. We receive a confirmation that it was bought (and its price and currency for our records), never your payment details.

When you write to us

If you email us (for example from Settings ▸ Support), we receive your email address and what you write. The email the game prepares also includes the game's version, your phone's platform and operating system, and your leaderboard ID, so we can look into your question; you can see and change all of it before sending. We use it only to answer you and keep it no longer than needed for that.

Notifications

If you allow them, the game shows local reminders (for example, that customers are waiting or the daily puzzle is ready). They're scheduled on your phone; no information is sent for them.

Your choices

  • Consent: in the EEA, the UK and US states that require it, the game asks for your consent with Google's consent form before any ad. Change your answers any time: Settings ▸ Privacy choices.
  • Tracking (iPhone): Apple's prompt asks whether apps may track you; you can change it in your iPhone's Settings ▸ Privacy & Security ▸ Tracking.
  • Advertising ID (Android): you can reset or delete it in your phone's Settings ▸ Privacy ▸ Ads.
  • Leaderboard: delete it in the game, as above.
  • Other requests (access, correction, deletion of data held by us or our providers): write to gamedelics@gmail.com with your leaderboard ID (shown in the support email the game prepares).

Who we share information with

Only the service providers above, for the purposes above: Unity Technologies (Unity Gaming Services, LevelPlay, Unity Ads), Google (Firebase, AdMob, the consent form), Tenjin, and the ad networks LevelPlay works with. We don't sell your personal information for money. Some of these providers process data outside your country, under their own safeguards. Their policies: Unity, Google, Tenjin.

How long information is kept

Firebase Analytics keeps event data for 14 months. Your leaderboard account is kept until you delete it or the game is retired. Crash reports and ad data are kept by each provider according to its own policy.

Children

Sushidoku is meant for players aged 13 and over and isn't directed at children. If you believe a child under 13 has used the leaderboard, write to us and we'll delete the account.

Changes

We'll update this page when the game's data practices change; the date at the top shows the latest version.
