Sushidoku Support

Help with Sushidoku, the cozy logic puzzle by Gamedelics

Write to us: gamedelics@gmail.com

The quickest way is from the game: Settings ▸ Support ▸ Email us. That email already includes your game version, your phone and your leaderboard ID, which helps us look into your question.

How to play

Place one sushi in every row, every column and every colour region, and never let two sushi touch, not even at the corners. Tap a cell to mark it with an X, and double-tap to place a sushi. Stuck? A Tip shows the next step and explains why it works.

Remove Ads, and restoring it

Remove Ads is a one-time purchase that removes the banner and the ads between levels. Reward videos you choose to watch stay. After reinstalling, or on a new phone with the same Google Play or Apple account, open Settings ▸ Restore Purchases.

Your leaderboard name

Change your name and your sushi in the Sushi Menu, on your name. To report another player's name, tap their row on the leaderboard and choose Report name; to stop seeing it, choose Hide this player.

Deleting your data

To delete your leaderboard account (your name, sushi and score): Sushi Menu ▸ your name ▸ Delete my leaderboard data. Your game progress on your phone stays as it is. To delete everything, uninstall the game. For any other request about your data, write to us at the address above. Our Privacy Policy has the details.

Privacy choices

Where the law requires it, the game asks for your consent before any ad. You can change your answers any time in Settings ▸ Privacy choices.
